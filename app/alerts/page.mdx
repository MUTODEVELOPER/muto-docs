# Alerts

<p className="doc-lead">MUTO Alerts is the live monitoring surface for signals produced by the ecosystem's intelligence pipelines. It converts changing conditions into a structured feed without turning every observation into a trading instruction.</p>

<div className="section-grid">
<div className="tech-panel"><span className="eyebrow">Workspace</span><h3>Live Signal Feed</h3><p>Run live scans, inspect matched or all observed signals, and review the latest available monitoring output from the application.</p></div>
<div className="tech-panel"><span className="eyebrow">Filtering</span><h3>Configurable Criteria</h3><p>Control signal type, priority and threshold filters such as minimum liquidity or volume where the underlying signal supports them.</p></div>
<div className="tech-panel"><span className="eyebrow">Delivery</span><h3>Web + Telegram</h3><p>Surface notifications inside the MUTO application and through Telegram delivery for time-sensitive monitoring workflows.</p></div>
<div className="tech-panel"><span className="eyebrow">Automation</span><h3>Recurring Scans</h3><p>The app can repeatedly scan for updated observations and preserve the last available result for continuity between checks.</p></div>
</div>

## Current Controls
<ul className="feature-list">
<li>Signal type and priority filtering</li>
<li>Minimum liquidity and volume thresholds where applicable</li>
<li>Live scan and last-result retrieval</li>
<li>Web notification delivery</li>
<li>Telegram notification delivery</li>
<li>Recurring live scanning inside the app</li>
</ul>

<Callout title="Alert design principle">Alerts surface observations for investigation and Agent context. A signal is not proof that an asset is safe, profitable or suitable for a user.</Callout>
