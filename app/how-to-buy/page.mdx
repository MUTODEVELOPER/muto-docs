# How to Buy $MUTO

<p className="doc-lead">
  $MUTO is available on BNB Chain. Use the official contract address and verify every destination before signing a transaction.
</p>

## Step 01 — Create a Wallet

Use a wallet that supports BNB Chain, such as MetaMask or Trust Wallet.

## Step 02 — Fund With BNB

Acquire BNB and send it to your wallet on the correct BNB Chain network.

## Step 03 — Open an Official Swap Route

Use the official MUTO site or a trusted DEX interface such as PancakeSwap.

## Step 04 — Verify the Contract

Paste and verify the official MUTO contract address before swapping.

## Step 05 — Swap BNB for $MUTO

Choose the amount, review the transaction and confirm the swap.

## Step 06 — Add the Token

If $MUTO does not appear automatically, add the token contract manually to your wallet.
